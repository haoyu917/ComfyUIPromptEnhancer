# BUFF AI V3.2 Audio Control Quick Guide

Recommended for MiniMax H3 short drama:

```text
audio_policy = Dialogue + Exact Offscreen
strict_speech_lock = true
```

`dialogue_text` is the exact on-screen speech whitelist. `offscreen_dialogue_text` is the exact off-screen speech whitelist. Under strict policies, all other sound design is treated as non-verbal.
